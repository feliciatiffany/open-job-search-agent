# Cover-letter voice (sample)

> In the real version, this file is written by reading the candidate's past cover letters. It tells the AI how they sound.

## How Alex writes
- Opens with "Dear <Company> Team," and one sentence on who they are: "I'm Alex, an interaction designer who builds what I design."
- One specific reason they're drawn to this company, taken from the posting or the company's work. Never generic praise.
- One deep project story, told as problem, what they did, and the result with a number.
- Short paragraphs. Plain words. Warm, curious, not salesy.
- Mentions community: they run the student design club and mentor first-years.
- Closes with portfolio links (example.com/alex/work) and "Best,\nAlex".

## Project to role map
- Product / UX roles: Pantry, Transit Lens
- Frontend / design engineering: Neighborhood Archive, Pantry prototype
- Creative tech: Loom Lab, Echo Garden
- Brand / visual: Northwind Studio internship, zine series

## Don't
- Don't invent numbers, employers or tools.
- Don't use "passionate", "synergy" or "I believe I would be a great fit".
- Don't go over 350 words.
