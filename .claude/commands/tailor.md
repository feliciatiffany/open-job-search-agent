Tailor an application for: $ARGUMENTS

1. Open the posting on the employer's own site. Note the role, level, must-haves, graduation window and the visa line.
2. Read profile/candidate.md and profile/resume.json. Pick the 3–4 projects and 4–5 experiences that fit best, and say why.
3. Suggest at most 3 swaps, using only lines that already exist in profile/resume.json.
4. Unless the posting forbids AI-written content, draft a cover letter in the candidate's voice (profile/voice.md), under 300 words, with every claim traceable to profile/resume.json or profile/facts.md.
5. Save it to applications/<company>-<role>.md.
